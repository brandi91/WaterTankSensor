Operating Cycle

From wake to deep sleep

Wake→ Connect Wi-Fi→ Sync time→ Measure→ Store history→ Publish MQTT→ Deep sleep
MQTT publishes only when enabled. Discovery is manual from the dashboard. NTP failure never blocks measurement. The web interface is normally inactive during deep sleep.
!

Electrical safety

Battery ADC requires a voltage divider. Never connect battery voltage or 5 V directly to an ESP32 ADC pin.
